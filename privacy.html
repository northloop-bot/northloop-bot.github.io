Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Autopilot ("the tool") is a private automation tool built and operated by a single individual (the "operator") to manage one YouTube channel that the operator owns. The tool is not distributed to, or used by, any other person.

What the tool accesses

The tool connects to the operator's own Google account via OAuth to:

  • upload videos to the operator's YouTube channel,
  • set video thumbnails and metadata (title, description),
  • schedule publish times,
  • read the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, audience retention).

What data is stored

The tool stores, on infrastructure controlled solely by the operator: the OAuth refresh token needed to act on the operator's own channel, and the files used to produce uploads. No data belonging to any third party is collected, stored, or shared. No data is sold or shared with any other party.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as the operator continues running the tool, and is deleted when the operator decommissions it or revokes the tool's access via Google Account permissions.

Contact

This tool has a single operator and no public users. Questions about this policy can be directed to the account that registered this domain.
